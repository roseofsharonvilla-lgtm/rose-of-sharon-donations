Why it matters

Why our residents need you

Many of the seniors who come to Rose of Sharon arrive with almost nothing. A bag of clothes, maybe a few photos, and a lot of uncertainty about what comes next.

Some have outlived their families. Others have simply lost touch. For many of them, there is no one coming to visit on Christmas morning.

We don't want anyone here to wake up on Christmas feeling forgotten.

This year we want every resident to have a stocking with their name on it, a gift picked just for them, and a home that feels warm and full. A new pair of socks, a favorite lotion, a card that says "you are loved." Little things mean more than you'd think.

You don't have to do it all. Pick one way to help below, and you'll be part of someone's Christmas.
